I currently work in quantitative trading, where I have built and led research and engineering teams developing automated trading systems across a range of asset classes.

My academic research interests range from the mathematical foundations of error-correcting codes to their use in distributed storage. On the theoretical side, I have worked on fundamental questions about the list decoding of random linear and LDPC codes; on the applied side, I have designed practical erasure codes for vast-scale distributed storage at hyperscalers. I received my PhD in Computer Science from Stanford University, where I was fortunate to be advised by Mary Wootters.
